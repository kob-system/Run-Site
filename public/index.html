What JobTally does

  • Crew GPS time clock — your guys clock in and out with one tap, both ends GPS-stamped, so you know they were on the job when they started and when they finished.
  • Receipt scanning — snap a photo at the register and JobTally reads the store and the total, then books it to the job.
  • Live per-job profit — every job shows what you're charging, what's gone out in labor and materials, and what's left for you — while the job is still running, not months later.
  • Estimates → invoices → paid — write the estimate on your phone, turn a "yes" into a job with one tap, and always see who still owes you.
  • Plus mileage tracking, job photos & daily logs, schedule & time off, reports & tax exports — all included.
