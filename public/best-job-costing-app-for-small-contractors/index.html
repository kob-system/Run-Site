Best job costing app for small contractors (2026)

There's no single "best" app for everyone — it depends on whether you want the cheapest time clock, a focused profit-tracking tool, or a full construction platform. Here's an honest read on nine of them, including the ones that beat JobTally on price, and how to pick.

The short version: If you only need hours tracked, Connecteam is free up to 10 users and OnTheClock is a few dollars a head — both cheaper than JobTally, and we'll say so plainly. If you need to prove exactly where a crew stood all day, Workyard and ClockShark have the strongest GPS and geofencing. If you run custom builds with client portals and change orders, Buildertrend is the platform. JobTally is for the owner-operator with a 2–10 person crew whose actual problem is not knowing which jobs made money — flat $150/month, unlimited crew, with job costing, receipt scanning, estimates and invoices all in the base price. How to pick — four questions

  • Is job costing included, or locked behind a tier? If profit-per-job is the reason you're buying, make sure it's in the plan you're paying for. In JobTally it's the base product.
  • Does the price climb with each crew member? Per-seat pricing can quietly balloon on a growing crew. JobTally is flat with unlimited crew.
  • How do labor and material costs get in? If it's manual entry, it won't happen. GPS clock-in and receipt scanning (both in JobTally) capture costs without extra work.
  • Do you see profit live or after the fact? Real-time per-job margin lets you fix a bleeding job before it's done — that's the whole point of job costing.

What it actually costs at your crew size

Per-seat pricing looks cheap on a small crew and stops looking cheap around the point where you'd need a second truck. Here's roughly where a flat $150/month passes the per-user plans, using pricing checked August 2026:

  • ClockShark Standard ($40 + $8/user) — cheaper than JobTally until about a 14-person crew.
  • ClockShark Pro ($60 + $10/user) — cheaper until about a 9-person crew.
  • Workyard Time Tracking ($50 + $8/user) — cheaper until about a 12-person crew.
  • Workyard Workforce Management ($50 + $16/user) — JobTally is cheaper from about 7 people up.
  • Connecteam — free up to 10 users, so cheaper than JobTally outright on a small crew.
  • OnTheClock (~$5 + ~$4/employee) — much cheaper at any crew size a small contractor is likely to run.
  • Buildertrend — quoted on annual construction volume, historically several hundred dollars a month, so JobTally is far cheaper.

The honest read: JobTally is not the cheapest way to punch a clock, and we're not going to pretend it is. It's priced as a job-costing tool — labor, materials and the quoted budget in one place with live profit — rather than as a time clock. If hours are all you need, buy the cheap time clock.
