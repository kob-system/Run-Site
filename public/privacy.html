JobTally is a job-costing and time-tracking tool for small contractors and their crews.

We wrote this in plain words on purpose. If anything is unclear, email support@getjobtally.com.

Workers, please read this: your location is stamped when you clock in and when you clock out

If a business owner invited you to JobTally as a worker, the app saves your phone's GPS location at the moment you clock in and the moment you clock out. The owner who invited you can see it in the app, and it is in the email the owner gets when you clock in or out. See Section 3 for exactly what is saved.

1. Who we are & who uses JobTally

JobTally has two kinds of users:

  • Business owners. They create an account, set up their company, add jobs and clients, and invite workers. Owners pay for JobTally once they run more than one job at a time.
  • Workers (crew). An owner invites them to clock in and out and see their own jobs. Workers use JobTally for free.

For the business data an owner enters about their company, jobs, clients, and workers, the business owner is the "controller" of that data. JobTally is a service provider / processor that handles it for the owner. For account information and for running the Service itself, JobTally is a controller. If you are a worker with questions about how a company uses your data, you can also ask the owner who invited you.

2. Information we collect

a. Account information

Your name, email address, and, for owners, your company name. If you set a password, our login provider (Supabase) stores it only in hashed form. We cannot read it. Workers usually sign in with an invite link instead of a password.

b. Location

For workers, the GPS location at clock-in and at clock-out. See Section 3.

c. Business data the owner enters

  • Jobs: names, addresses, stage, budgets, prices, and notes.
  • Client details the owner types in, such as a customer's name, phone, email, and address.
  • Estimates, invoices, change orders, and payments.
  • Workers' pay rates, hours, labor cost, and pay records.
  • Mileage, schedules, time off requests, shopping and fix-it lists, daily notes, permits, warranty callbacks, and insurance and license records.
  • Messages in each job's crew chat.

d. Photos & files

Receipt photos, job photos, job documents, and insurance or license files that owners or workers upload. They are kept in a private storage bucket, not on a public web address. See Section 9 for how receipt photos are read.

e. What you ask the assistant

What you type or say to the Ask assistant, and a log of every change the assistant made (what it changed and who asked for it). See Section 9.

f. Billing information

Stripe handles card payments. We keep your Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, plan, subscription status, and renewal date. We do not receive or store card numbers. See Section 8.

g. Product usage and signup source

  • Usage events. When you do certain things in the app (open it, create a job, add a receipt, reach the payment screen), we save an event in our own database. Each event holds your account ID (once you are signed in), a random ID stored in your browser, the event name, and a few small details such as which screen or which plan. We do not put names, emails, addresses, or dollar amounts in these events. They are tied to your account, not just added up.
  • Signup source. If you arrived through a tagged link, an ad, or a partner's link, we save which one (the link's tags, the partner name, the page you landed on, and when you first visited) with your new account.

h. Technical information

Like any website, our hosts see your IP address and browser type when your phone or computer talks to the Service. If the app crashes, it sends us an error report by email with the error, the page, your account ID and role, the app version, and your browser type.

3. Worker location

What is saved: your phone's latitude and longitude at the moment you tap clock in, and again at the moment you tap clock out on the clock screen. Your browser asks your permission first.

Why: so the owner can see that time was recorded at or near the job and can cost labor to the right job.

Who sees it: the owner who invited you. It shows in the owner's app, and the email the owner gets when you clock in or out includes a map link to that spot. You can see your own. JobTally looks at it only if needed to run or fix the Service.

What is not saved: anything between clock-in and clock-out. There is no background tracking and nothing while you are off the clock. Clocking in or out by asking the assistant saves no location at all.

If you block location, or your phone cannot get a signal, your shift still saves. It just has no location. Your employer decides to use JobTally for time tracking, not JobTally. If location capture bothers you, talk to the owner who invited you.

4. How we use information

  • Run the Service: accounts, time tracking, job costing, estimates, invoices, schedules, and crew chat.
  • Show owners their crew's hours and clock-in and clock-out locations.
  • Read receipt photos and run the assistant, using Anthropic (Section 9).
  • Handle subscription billing through Stripe.
  • Send email: clock-in and clock-out alerts to the owner, welcome emails, a weekly summary to owners, an alert to the owner when a worker is waiting to be put on a job, payment receipts and failed-payment notices, and account emails such as password resets.
  • Answer support requests.
  • Find and fix bugs, keep the Service secure, and improve it. Usage events and signup source are used for this.
  • Follow the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your job, client, location, or money data to show you ads, and there are no ads in JobTally.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the Service you or your employer signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure, fix, and improve the Service and prevent abuse, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: where required, for example the location permission you grant in your browser, which you can take back.
  • Legal obligation: to keep records the law requires.

For worker data handled for an owner, the owner is responsible for having a legal basis and for telling workers about location capture where their local law requires it.

6. Who can see what inside JobTally

  • Other contractors: nothing. The database itself checks who is asking on every request, so one business cannot read another business's jobs, clients, crew, or files.
  • Owners see everything in their own account, including their crew's hours, pay, clock-in and clock-out locations, and job chat.
  • Workers see:
    • their own shifts, hours, and pay, and their own schedule and time off;
    • the jobs they are assigned to: the job name, address, and stage, but not the client's phone number or email, the budget, or the price;
    • the shopping and fix-it lists, crew chat, and photos for those jobs;
    • the names of the crewmates on those jobs.
    A worker can add a receipt to a job he is on, but cannot see receipts. Workers cannot see other workers' pay, job budgets or profit, estimates, or invoices.
  • JobTally. One person runs JobTally. He can reach account data to keep the Service working, to fix a problem, or to answer a request you make.

8. Payments & card data

Subscriptions are billed through Stripe. You enter card details on Stripe's page, and they go to Stripe. JobTally never receives or stores your card number, security code, or full card details. We send Stripe your email address and your JobTally account ID so it can match the payment to your account. We keep your Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, plan, status, and renewal date. Stripe's handling of your payment data is covered by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

9. AI: receipt scanning & the assistant

JobTally uses Anthropic's Claude for two things. Anthropic handles this data under Anthropic's commercial terms, and we use it only to do what you asked.

Receipt scanning

When you add a receipt photo, we send the image to Anthropic to read what is printed on it: the store, date, subtotal, tax, and total. You see the result and can change it before saving.

The Ask assistant

When you ask the assistant something, by typing or talking, your message goes to Anthropic. So does the account data the assistant looks up to answer, which can include your jobs, your clients and their contact details, prices and budgets, crew names, hours and pay rates, estimates, and invoices. When a worker uses the assistant, it looks up only his own hours, schedule, and jobs. When the assistant wants to change something, it shows a confirm card first, and nothing is saved until you tap Confirm. Every change it makes is logged in your account.

AI can get things wrong. Check scanned amounts and assistant answers before you rely on them. See the disclaimers in our Terms of Service.

10. Voice input

The microphone button uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your browser, the audio may be sent to the browser's maker to turn it into text (for example, Google in Chrome, or Apple in Safari). JobTally only gets the text, never the audio. If you would rather not use it, type instead.

11. Keeping & deleting data

While your account is open

We keep your data as long as the account is open. Cancelling your subscription does not delete anything. The account drops to the free plan and your records stay.

When an owner deletes their account

Owners can delete their account in the app, under Settings → Delete my account. You type your own email to confirm. Then:

  1. Billing stops first. Any live Stripe subscription is canceled. If we cannot confirm billing has stopped, nothing is deleted and we ask you to email us.
  2. Your records are deleted: jobs and client details, estimates, invoices, change orders, receipts, hours, pay records, mileage, schedules, time off requests, lists, daily notes, permits, warranty callbacks, insurance and license records, job chat, the assistant's change log, your usage events and signup source, your subscription record, and your login.
  3. Receipt photos you uploaded yourself are deleted from storage.

Not yet removed by the delete button: job photos, job documents, insurance and license files, and receipt photos your crew added. Those files stay in private storage. Email support@getjobtally.com and we will delete them. If you want to be sure every file is gone, email us after you delete.

Your workers' logins are theirs, so they are not deleted. They are unlinked from your company. Their hours on your jobs are deleted along with your jobs.

When a worker's account is deleted

Workers cannot delete their own account from the app yet. Email support@getjobtally.com and we will do it. Deleting a worker's account also deletes his hours, pay records, schedule, and time off requests, including the copies in his boss's account. Owners should download their data first if they need those records. Chat messages the worker wrote, and photos and receipts he added to a job, stay in the owner's account.

What can remain after a delete

  • Copies in our database host's daily backups, for up to about 8 days, until those backups roll off.
  • Emails already sent, including the notice we get that an account was closed.
  • Stripe's own records of your customer account and past payments.
  • Anything we must keep by law.

An owner controls the worker and client data in their own account and can change or delete it there.

12. Security

  • HTTPS only. Every connection is encrypted, and the site refuses to load any other way.
  • Database rules on every request. Row-level security in the database decides which rows each login can read, as described in Section 6.
  • Private file storage. Photos and files are in a private bucket. The app gives short-lived links only to people allowed to see them.
  • Hashed passwords. We cannot read your password.
  • Limited access. Only the one person who runs JobTally can reach account data.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security. If we learn of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

13. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of these rights:

  • Access / a copy. Owners can download their whole account any time from Settings → Your data → Download everything I have. It is a spreadsheet file with every record, plus links to job photos, receipt photos, and documents that work for 7 days. Anyone can also ask us for a copy.
  • Correction. Fix wrong information. Owners and workers can edit much of it in the app.
  • Deletion. Owners can delete their account in the app (Section 11). Workers, or anyone who prefers, can email us.
  • Objection / restriction. Ask us to stop or limit certain uses.
  • Withdraw consent. For example, turn off location permission for the site in your browser.

To make a request, email support@getjobtally.com. We will confirm it is you and answer within the time the law requires. Workers: some requests are for the owner who controls your data, and we will help route them. We will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

14. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose it; to request access to it and deletion of it; to correct it; and to be free from discrimination for using these rights.

We do not "sell" your personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as the CCPA/CPRA defines those terms. What we collect and why is in Sections 2 to 4. Who receives it is in Section 7. To use your rights, email support@getjobtally.com. You may use an authorized agent, subject to verification.

15. EU/UK/EEA rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the rights in Section 13 under the GDPR or UK GDPR, and the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Our legal bases are in Section 5. JobTally is built for U.S. contractors, and your data is stored in the United States (Section 18). For any GDPR request, email support@getjobtally.com.

16. Cookies, browser storage & usage data

No ad trackers and no outside analytics. The site and app load no third-party scripts. We do not use advertising cookies.

The app keeps a few things in your browser's own storage:

  • Your sign-in, so you stay signed in. If you uncheck "stay signed in" when you log in, it is cleared when you close the app.
  • A random ID for your browser, used with the usage events in Section 2g.
  • The link tags from your first visit, if any, for signup source.
  • A few settings, such as whether the assistant reads answers out loud.

You can clear these in your browser settings. Clearing your sign-in will sign you out.
