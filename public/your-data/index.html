Your data

It’s your business. It’s your data.

Your jobs, your crew’s hours, your client list and your receipts are the record of how you make a living. Here’s exactly where it lives, who can see it, and how you get it out or wipe it, without asking anyone’s permission.

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Straight answer: one tap in Settings exports your entire account to a spreadsheet, with links to your photos, receipts and documents. One button in Settings stops your billing and deletes your account and records. No other contractor can see your rows, and JobTally makes money from the subscription, not from your data.

Get everything out: one tap, no ticket

In Settings, owners have a button that says “Download everything I have.” It builds one spreadsheet file covering your whole account: jobs, clients, estimates, invoices, change orders, hours, receipts, mileage, schedule, lists, notes, permits, insurance and licenses, time off, crew pay, and your crew.

Photos and files can’t sit inside a spreadsheet, so each job photo, receipt photo and document gets a download link in the file. The links stop working 7 days after the export, on purpose, because a permanent link to your receipts is a leak waiting to happen. Run the export again any time for fresh links.

You don’t email anybody for this and nobody approves it. It’s a button.

Delete it: also a button

Owners have a two-step delete in Settings. You type your own email address to confirm. It only ever deletes the account you’re signed in to. There is no way to point it at somebody else.

If you’re the owner

Billing is canceled first. If we can’t confirm it stopped, nothing is deleted. Then your jobs, clients, receipts, hours, pay records, estimates, invoices and the rest of your records go, along with your login and the receipt photos you took.

Job photos, job documents, insurance and license files, and receipts your crew added are not wiped by the button yet. Email support@getjobtally.com and we delete them.

Your crew’s logins are theirs, so they aren’t deleted. They’re unlinked from you. Their hours on your jobs go with your jobs.

If you’re a worker

There’s no delete button on the crew side yet. Email support@getjobtally.com and we’ll delete your account.

Deleting a worker also deletes his hours, pay records, schedule and time off, including in his boss’s account. Bosses: download your data first if you need those records. Chat messages he wrote, and photos and receipts he added, stay with the job.

After a delete, copies stay in the database host’s daily backups for up to about 8 days, then roll off. Emails already sent stay sent, and Stripe keeps its own record of past payments.

Export first if you want a copy. After a delete there is nothing to hand back.

Who can see your numbers

Other contractors: no.

Accounts aren’t separated by what the screens choose to show you. They’re separated in the database. Rules sitting on the data itself decide which rows each login is allowed to read, so a request for another company’s job comes back empty even if someone goes around the app entirely.

Your crew: only their own side.

A worker sees his own shifts, hours and pay, and the jobs he’s on: the job name, address and stage, plus that job’s shopping and fix-it lists, crew chat, photos, and the names of the crewmates on it. He does not see the client’s phone or email, the budget, the price, receipts, invoices, or anyone else’s pay. You, the owner, see everything in your account.

Workers’ location: two moments, not all day.

GPS is stamped when a worker clocks in and when he clocks out. Nothing in between, ever. If location is blocked, the shift still saves without it. Workers see that stated plainly, in the app and in the privacy policy.

JobTally: only to run it and fix it.

One person runs this company. He can reach account data to keep the app working and to fix something you report, the same as at any small software company. Better that you hear it here than assume otherwise.

How it’s kept

Stored in the U.S.

Your data sits with Supabase, on Amazon Web Services in Northern California.

Encrypted on the way

Every connection is HTTPS and the site refuses to load any other way. Passwords are stored hashed. Nobody at JobTally can read yours.

Receipts aren’t public

Receipt photos, job photos and documents sit in private storage, not on an open web address. The app hands out short-lived links to the person who’s allowed to see them.

Nothing sold, nothing advertised

JobTally makes money one way: the subscription. Your data isn’t sold, rented, or turned into ad targeting.

What we track about how you use it

No ad trackers and no outside analytics. The site loads no third-party scripts.

We do keep our own record of what gets used, in our own database, tied to your account: things like “opened the app,” “created a job,” “added a receipt.” No names, emails, addresses or dollar amounts go in it. We also note which link, ad or partner brought you in when you signed up. We use both to fix the app and make it better, and both are deleted with your account.

What happens if you cancel

Cancelling stops the billing. It doesn’t wipe your account and it doesn’t hold your history hostage. Export it whenever you like. If you want it gone as well as stopped, use the delete button. Both are yours to press. Neither needs a phone call.
